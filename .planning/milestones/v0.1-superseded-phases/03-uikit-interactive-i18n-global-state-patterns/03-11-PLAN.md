<objective>
Close GAP-02 + GAP-04 (blockers) with ONE motion policy. GAP-02: overlay/Toast entrance animations do not play at runtime — the recipe classes (`transition duration-150 data-[state=closed]:scale-95/opacity-0`) exist, but content mounts directly in `data-state="open"` (opacity 1 / transform none on mount), so the closed→open frame is never rendered; overlays also hardcode `duration-150` instead of the `--duration-*`/`--ease-*` motion tokens [src: 03-UAT.md#GAP-02] [src: 03-UAT-VISUAL-FINDINGS.md#Runtime-verification]. GAP-04: ToastManager declares `--gap:12px` + `overlap:true` but applies neither the gap nor the overlap transforms — fired toasts stack flush (gaps [0,0,0], `transform:none`, `transitionDuration:0s`), no enter/exit, nothing fans out on hover [src: 03-UAT.md#GAP-04] [src: 03-UAT-VISUAL-FINDINGS.md#TM1/TM4].

Purpose: introduce ONE motion policy (duration/easing tokens + a mount enter-frame mechanism + a `prefers-reduced-motion` opt-out) applied across the whole overlay+toast family, and make toast stacking real.

Output: a working enter/exit animation across Dialog/Menu/Popover/Tooltip/Toast, real toast stacking, and a motion regression test that fails on the dead pre-fix mount transition.
</objective>
